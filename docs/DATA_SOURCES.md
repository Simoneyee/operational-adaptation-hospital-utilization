# Data Sources

Raw data are not redistributed in this repository.

## U.S. Department of Health and Human Services

**COVID-19 Reported Patient Impact and Hospital Capacity by Facility**

Used for hospital-level admissions, staffed adult inpatient beds, occupied adult inpatient beds, ICU capacity, COVID inpatient census, and reporting-coverage fields.

The final empirical period is July 2020 through December 2022.

## National Center for Health Statistics

**Excess Deaths Associated With COVID-19: National and State Estimates of Excess Deaths**

Used in secondary state-level mortality analyses.

## CDC county-week COVID-19 mortality

Used in the Health Service Area mortality sensitivity analysis. Small-count suppression materially limits this analysis.

## NCI/SEER Health Service Area crosswalk and population

Used for substate aggregation and denominator construction.

## Federal military medical-support deployment inventory

Used only for an external intervention-aligned sensitivity analysis. The matched event-study design failed the pretrend criterion and is not interpreted causally.

## Redistribution policy

This repository intentionally does not include raw public-source files. Reproducibility scripts expect the analyst to obtain the source datasets directly from the original providers.
